Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Guild Raid Tracker (the "Service") is an invite-only tool that tracks raid activity for a Wynncraft guild and works out in-game rune payouts. The Service is designed to need as little personal data as possible. This page explains what we do use, why, and how to use your GDPR rights.

Who is responsible

The Service is run by GoSkyHigh, who is the controller of your personal data.

Service guild-raid-tracker.netlify.app
Contact cx0ypaexy@mozmail.com

What we process

About people who use the Service (from Discord and from what you do in the Service)

Data Why
Your account record: Discord ID, username and avatar link, received when you log in (we ask only for the identify scope), plus whether you are an admin, when you were added and when you last logged in Log you in, show who you are and manage who has access
Payouts you record: the member, raid, day, count and time, plus your Discord ID and username. Other logged-in users can see who recorded a payout, and your Discord ID is included in the data the Service sends to their browser Prevent double payouts and keep a record of who was paid
Server logs held by our hosting providers: IP address, browser details and error messages (which can include parts of what Discord or Wynncraft sent back) Deliver and secure the Service

Data about guild players comes from the Wynncraft API and is covered in the Terms of Service.

We do not require an email address, password, phone number, payment details or precise location to use the Service, and we do not request GPS location. Our hosting providers see your IP address, which can reveal your approximate location. If you email us, we process your address and message to reply. We do not keep any Discord login token: the one Discord gives us is used once during login and then discarded. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not sell data or build advertising profiles.

Why we may use it (legal basis)

We rely on legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): running the guild's raid-reward system fairly and securely, with a record of who was paid. We keep the data to what that needs. You can object at any time (see Your rights).

Do you have to give us your data?

Logging in requires your Discord ID and username. Without them you cannot use the Service. You do not have to give us anything else.

Cookies and local storage

  • jwt cookie: keeps you logged in for up to 30 days. It contains your Discord ID, an expiry time and a session version number. It is signed so it cannot be altered, but it is not encrypted. It is strictly necessary for logging in. It is marked HttpOnly, so scripts on the page cannot read the cookie itself.
  • Login token in memory: when you log in, the same token is also passed to the page and held in your browser's memory while the page is open, and it is sent with requests to the Service. It is not saved to your device's storage.
  • theme in local storage: remembers light or dark mode. It stays in your browser and is never sent to us.

There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

Who else sees your data

  • Discord handles your login. Your browser also loads avatar images from Discord's CDN, so Discord sees your IP address.
  • Netlify hosts the website and relays your requests to the API, Neon hosts the database and FastAPI Cloud runs the API. They process data on our behalf and keep server logs that include IP addresses.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy: Discord, Netlify, Neon (part of Databricks) and FastAPI Cloud.

Transfers outside the EEA

Our website, database and API run on US-based providers (the database and API in us-east-1). Where your data goes to the US, these are the safeguards we rely on, and you can read each one yourself:

  • Netlify (website): certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (check the listing). Its Data Processing Agreement adds Standard Contractual Clauses as a fallback if that framework stops applying.
  • Neon (database): Neon is part of Databricks. Its Data Processing Addendum uses Standard Contractual Clauses. Neon's own terms are at https://neon.com/platform-terms.
  • FastAPI Cloud (API): its privacy policy says it hosts in the US and may transfer data under a data processing agreement with standard clauses, but it does not publish that agreement, so we cannot link it here.

How long we keep data

Nothing is deleted automatically. We keep data as follows:

  • Your account record (Discord ID, username, avatar link, admin status and timestamps) is kept until your access is removed.
  • Payout records are kept for as long as the Service is in use, because they are the record of who was paid. The Discord ID and username of whoever recorded a payout stay on it until that person asks us to remove them. A payout that is voided is deleted.
  • Your login cookie lasts 30 days.
  • Our hosting providers keep their own server logs and backups for the periods they set.

To ask us to delete your data, see Your rights.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to show you your data, correct it, erase it, restrict its use, give you a copy, or stop using it (object). Email cx0ypaexy@mozmail.com and we will answer within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity, for example by contacting us from your Discord account.

Some limits apply: we keep the payout records themselves (what was paid and when) as the record of who was paid. If you ask us to erase your data, we delete your account record and replace your Discord ID and username on payouts you recorded with "removed user". If something we hold is wrong, tell us and we will fix or remove it.

You can also complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live or work. In Romania, that is the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP): https://www.dataprotection.ro.

Age

Security

Access is invite-only, connections use HTTPS, and we never see or store your Discord password. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authority as the law requires.
