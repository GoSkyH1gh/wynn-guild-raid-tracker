Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This service collects very little personal data: no passwords, no analytics, no ads. This page explains what we do use, why, and how to use your GDPR rights.

Who is responsible

Guild Raid Tracker is run by GoSkyHigh, who is the controller of your personal data.

Website guild-raid-tracker.netlify.app
Contact cx0ypaexy@mozmail.com

What we process

About people who use the app (from Discord and from what you do in the app)

Data Why
Discord ID, username and avatar link, received when you log in (we ask only for the identify scope) Log you in and show who you are
Payouts you record: the member, raid, day, count and time, plus your Discord ID and username Prevent double payouts and keep a record of who was paid
IP address and browser details, held in our hosting providers' server logs Deliver and secure the service

About guild players (from Wynncraft's public API, whether or not they use the app)

Data Why
In-game username, UUID and guild rank, with membership timestamps Identify guild members and check payout eligibility
Raid completion counts, saved every 30 minutes, and the completions we detect from changes Calculate payouts
Payout records: what was paid, for which raid and day Prevent double payouts and keep a record of who was paid

Player data is public on Wynncraft. We store our own history of it over time, which Wynncraft does not show.

We do not require an email address, password, phone number, payment details or precise location to use the service, and we do not request GPS location. Our hosting providers see your IP address, which can reveal your approximate location. If you email us, we process your address and message to reply. We do not store your Discord access token; it is used once during login. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not sell data or build advertising profiles.

Why we may use it (legal basis)

We rely on legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): running the guild's raid-reward system fairly and securely, with a record of who was paid. We keep the data to what that needs. You can object at any time (see Your rights).

If you are a guild member but never log in

We still hold your in-game username, UUID, rank and raid counts, because we get them from Wynncraft and not from you. This policy is your notice for that. You can ask what we hold, or ask to be removed, at any time.

Cookies and local storage

  • jwt cookie: keeps you logged in for up to 30 days. It is strictly necessary for logging in, so it needs no consent banner, and it is HttpOnly so scripts cannot read it.
  • theme in local storage: remembers light or dark mode. It stays in your browser and is never sent to us.

There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

Who else sees your data

  • Discord handles your login. Your browser also loads avatar images from Discord's CDN, so Discord sees your IP address.
  • Wynncraft: we send them the guild ID and our API key and receive roster and raid data. None of your personal data goes to them.
  • Netlify hosts the website, Neon hosts the database and FastAPI Cloud runs the API. They process data on our behalf, and FastAPI Cloud temporarily keeps IP addresses in its server logs.
  • Google Fonts: the site loads its font from Google, so Google sees your IP address and browser details.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Transfers outside the EEA

Our database and API run in the US (us-east-1), and Discord, Netlify and Google are US companies. Where your data leaves the EU, we rely on the transfer mechanism each provider offers, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep data

We do not delete data automatically. We keep it for as long as the guild uses this tracker, so we can keep a record of who was paid and sort out any mix-ups. When you ask, we delete the data we hold about you, except for the payout records described in Your rights. Deletion is done by hand, so it can take a little time.

Your login cookie lasts 30 days. Our hosting providers keep their own server logs and backups for the periods they set.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to show you your data, correct it, erase it, restrict its use, give you a copy, or stop using it (object). Email cx0ypaexy@mozmail.com and we will answer within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity, for example by contacting us from your Discord account.

Some limits apply: we may keep basic payout records (what was paid, for which raid and day, and who recorded it) so the ledger stays accurate and mix-ups can be sorted out. Removing your app access does not delete those records. When a payout is voided, its record is deleted immediately and is not kept. If something we hold is wrong, tell us and we will fix or remove it. Errors in data that comes from Wynncraft may also need fixing at Wynncraft.

You can also complain to the data protection authority in the country where you live or work. In Romania, that is the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP): https://www.dataprotection.ro.

Automated calculations

The app suggests payout amounts using fixed rules (rank, daily caps and payout cycles). A person decides and records each payout, and a payout can be voided.

Age

The service is invite-only. You must meet Discord's minimum age in your country (13 or higher).

Security

Access is invite-only, connections use HTTPS, the login cookie is HttpOnly, and we never see or store your Discord password. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authority as the law requires.

Changes

We will update this page when something changes and change the date at the top.

Terms of Use

Last updated: 28 October 2026

By using Guild Raid Tracker you agree to these short terms, which cover who can use Guild Raid Tracker, how payouts work and what we cannot promise.

Who can use it

The service is invite-only. Admins add and remove users, and you log in with Discord. You must meet Discord's minimum age in your country (13 or higher), and you are responsible for keeping your Discord account secure.

Roles

  • Members can view tracker, reward, payout and status data, refresh the data, record payouts, and void payouts they recorded themselves.
  • Admins can also manage users, change daily caps and payout cycles, and void any payout.

Acceptable use

Do not scrape the service, try to access it without permission, interfere with it, or record payouts that did not happen.

How payouts work

  • Only certain guild ranks are eligible (currently Recruit, Recruiter and Captain). Other ranks are tracked but not paid.
  • Each eligible raid completion earns one rune, up to a daily cap per raid type. Oldest days are paid first.
  • Payouts can only be recorded for a payout cycle that has ended and whose payout window is still open (currently 7 days).
  • Admins can change caps and cycle settings, and a change applies to all dates at once.
  • Eligibility is worked out from current ranks and current caps. A rank or cap change can therefore change what the app shows as owed for past days. Payouts already recorded do not change.
  • To claim your runes, contact a guild officer (Duke or above) before the payout window closes.
  • Voiding a payout permanently deletes its record; no history of the void is kept.

Data accuracy

Raid data comes from the Wynncraft API and is refreshed every 30 minutes. If a player restricts API access, their raid data may be missing. A completion's time is when the app noticed it, not necessarily when the raid happened. We do not guarantee the data is complete or correct. If you think something is wrong, contact cx0ypaexy@mozmail.com.

Runes have no cash value

Payouts are in-game runes only. Nothing in this service has real-world monetary value, and nothing can be refunded or exchanged for money.
